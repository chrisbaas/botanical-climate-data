# TerraClimate 1991–2020 experimental monthly profiles

Published **separately** from the older WorldClim (1970–2000) archive.

This provider downloads immutable-global NetCDF files of tmin, tmax, ppt and pet from the Northwest Knowledge Network THREDDS fileServer (climatology/TerraClimate_19912020_VAR.nc), recording SHA256 of every file and Natural Earth land mask.

Each 0.25-degree cell is a nearest-native (~1/24 degree) grid-centre sample; **not an area average**. Monthly tmin/tmax are averages of daily minima/maxima; **not historical extreme nights**. Monthly mean temperature is approximated by averaging these two values. Monthly precipitation and TerraClimate's reference evapotranspiration (pet) are monthly totals in mm. The latter is NOT proven numerically equivalent to the FAO ET0 series used by the frozen C3d model. Public records have no botanical predictions.

The October 2026 TerraClimate website warns against mixing v1.0/v1.1 datasets; the generation of these legacy climatology files is therefore conservatively marked unknown pending confirmation. Colombia retains a precipitation scientific-hold flag. Do not repurpose as independently validated reference labels.

The published tile SHA256 provides tamper detection and reproducible archived outputs. Unattended scheduling begins only after live acquisition and independent import checks succeed.
